## Use the prompts well

- Start with a clear desired outcome, then give the agent enough project context to verify its work.
- Keep placeholders in `{braces}` until you replace them with your repository, file, issue, or operational context.
- Prefer prompts that ask the agent to test, compare, or verify the result.
